## A — ANCHOR (dán ĐẦU mọi prompt)
```
Rough low-budget educational doodle, single frame, primitive white stick-figure cartoon on plain white background, paper-white character body (NO skin tone fill), clean bold black marker outlines (confident, only slightly hand-drawn), simple round head, tiny dot eyes, one-line mouth, no nose, sparse messy black scribble hair,
```
## Z — LOCK (dán CUỐI mọi prompt)
```
flat minimal colors ONLY on animals/props/clothing/text; plain white background, no scenery, no gradients, no shadows, no textures; bold clear readable in one second; not cute mascot, not clean vector, no photorealism, no 3D, no watermark, no signature, 16:9.
```

- **[MODERN]** = `a modern white stick figure with short black hair, wearing a simple blue hoodie and blue jeans, plain white face with dot eyes,`
