Why do you jerk awake as you fall asleep? That sudden kick, the flailing arms, the feeling that you were falling. It has a name, a hypnic jerk, and the best explanation reaches deep into human evolution. For tens of millions of years, our ancient ancestors were primates who slept in trees, and falling meant death. This is the prehistoric, evolutionary reason your body still panics the moment you drift off, even in a safe bed.

The answer goes back to a reflex built to catch a falling ape, one that never switched off, even after ancient humans came down from the trees and started sleeping on the ground.
