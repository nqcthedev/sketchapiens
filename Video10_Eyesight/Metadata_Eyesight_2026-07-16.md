Why are your eyes worse than an ancient human's? A caveman could spot a deer on a hillside a mile away, at dusk, with no glasses, no surgery, nothing. You reach for a prescription before the world comes into focus. This is the real story of ancient human eyesight, evolution, and why nearsightedness (myopia) exploded in a single century.

It isn't just screens. It isn't just your genes. Your eyes are Stone Age eyes, and they grew up in a world they were never built for. The good news is the fix was never complicated. It's the same thing your ancestors' eyes drank in every day. For free.
