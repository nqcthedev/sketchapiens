So that is the answer.
They could not hold it until morning because on cold nights the body would not let them.
And the ones who could hold it least were small, or pregnant, or old.
That is not a story about bravery. Nobody chose any of it.
You will never take that walk, because there is nothing outside your bedroom.
But tonight you will get up in the dark and go to the bathroom.
Thirty seconds, on a floor you have crossed ten thousand times.
Watch what your legs do on the way back.
They speed up.
You will not decide to do that, and nothing in that house has ever chased anyone.
Nobody can prove where the hurry came from. Hurrying does not fossilise.
But somebody made that walk before you, on a cold night, and did not always come back.
And you are still walking a little faster, on a cold floor, on the way to bed.
