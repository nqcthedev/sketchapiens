# V19 — ĐỢT 3: CHƯƠNG 5 (khúc thành thật + cú lớn nhất)

```
Before the last part, one honest admission.
Nobody can measure how well a fire actually worked.
There is no study of Paleolithic campfires and how many predators they turned back.
Camping lore says a fire holds animals off. It might. Nobody has measured it.
And nobody can rerun the Stone Age to find out.
But there is no need to rerun it, because in some places it never stopped.
In the Haramaya district of eastern Ethiopia, between October 2011 and December 2013, three researchers worked through local records and interviewed households.
They were studying spotted hyenas, and how people and hyenas share the same ground.
They recorded twenty four attacks on people.
Half of those attacks happened while the person was going to the toilet in the evening.
The rest happened to people sleeping outdoors, or walking between villages after dark.
Between 2010 and 2012, twelve people in that district were killed.
Seven of them were children under the age of twelve.
That is the number worth sitting with. Half.
Not hunting. Not farming. Not travelling.
The most common thing a person was doing was stepping outside at night for the most ordinary reason there is.
And notice what that district does not have.
No mammoths. No stone tools. No cave.
It has roads. It has a hospital. It has electricity in some houses and not others.
What it shares with fifty thousand years ago is much smaller than any of that.
An animal that hunts at dusk. A place you have to leave. And no light on the path.
Those three things are older than farming, older than language, older than us.
And for almost all of human history, every single person alive had all three.
```

## Bốn lớp giáp chính sách — đối chiếu

| Lớp | Ở đâu |
|---|---|
| **1. Neo khảo cổ/địa danh mở đầu** | *"In the Haramaya district of eastern Ethiopia, between October 2011 and December 2013, three researchers…"* — địa danh + mốc + nhóm nghiên cứu, trước mọi con số |
| **2. Thuật ngữ trung tính** | *"recorded twenty four attacks"* · *"were killed"* — không tả vết thương, không tả cảnh |
| **3. Giọng nhân học khách quan** | không một chữ phán xét; hyena được gọi là loài chia sẻ cùng mặt đất, không phải quái vật |
| **4. Không tả thực** | *"Seven of them were children under the age of twelve"* — **một dòng, nói một lần, đi tiếp ngay.** Đọc như dòng thống kê trong bài báo |

## Đối chiếu C6 — khúc thành thật có RÚT LUI không?

Rubric cảnh báo: *"ta sẽ không bao giờ biết" đặt ngay trước payoff là tự bắn vào chân.*

Bốn dòng đầu **đúng là rút lui** *(không đo được lửa, không dựng lại được thời tiền sử)* —
nhưng dòng thứ sáu **lật ngược nó thành leo thang**: *"there is no need to rerun it, because
in some places it never stopped."* Rút lui biến thành cú đẩy. Đạt C6.

**Nguồn:** Dejene S.W., Dechassa N. & Reddy R.U. (2016), *International Journal of
Biodiversity and Conservation* 8(1): 1–7.
