# Ngân hàng ví dụ TRƯỚC → SAU (de-AI narration tiếng Anh)

Mỗi cặp: dòng AI-ish (trước) → dòng người-thật (sau). Cùng nghĩa, cùng sự thật,
thường còn NGẮN hơn. Dùng làm "tai mẫu", không phải để chép nguyên văn.

## Bỏ rào đón / đằng hắng
- ❌ It's important to note that the brain uses a lot of energy.
- ✅ Your brain is a fuel hog.

- ❌ It's worth mentioning that this process took millions of years.
- ✅ This took millions of years.

## Bỏ intensifier rỗng + đại ngôn
- ❌ This was a truly remarkable and incredibly important adaptation.
- ✅ This one change rewired everything.

- ❌ Fire was a game-changer that played a crucial role in human evolution.
- ✅ Fire changed what humans could eat — and how big their brains could get.

## Phá rule-of-three / câu cân đối
- ❌ They hunted, they gathered, and they survived against all odds.
- ✅ They hunted. They gathered. Mostly, they just refused to die.

## Bỏ khuôn "not only… but also"
- ❌ Sweat not only cools the body but also allows for endurance hunting.
- ✅ Sweat cooled them down. That's also how they ran prey into the ground.

## Bỏ mở/đóng bài sáo
- ❌ So buckle up, because we're about to dive into the story of human hair.
- ✅ So why did you lose your fur? It starts with heat.

- ❌ In conclusion, humans lost their fur for many reasons.
- ✅ So there wasn't one reason you went bald all over. There were five.

## Bỏ "kicker/plot twist/little did they know"
- ❌ But here's the kicker: the baby grows fur before it's even born.
- ✅ And before you were born? You grew a full coat. Then shed it.

## Bỏ giọng Wikipedia / bị động
- ❌ It is believed by scientists that body lice evolved around 170,000 years ago.
- ✅ Body lice show up around 170,000 years ago. That's our clue.

## Bỏ "—" và ";" (TTS đọc vấp)
- ❌ The skin darkened — a built-in sunscreen; it protected the body.
- ✅ The skin darkened. Built-in sunscreen.

## Tạo nhịp: thả câu cụt sau câu dài
- ❌ Despite lacking claws, sharp teeth, or any real natural weapons, humans managed to become the most dangerous animal on the planet.
- ✅ No claws. No fangs. No armor. And somehow you ended up the most dangerous animal alive.

## Cụ thể thắng trừu tượng
- ❌ Humans developed a sophisticated thermoregulatory system.
- ✅ You're basically a walking radiator with millions of tiny sweat taps.

## Giữ contractions / nói với "you"
- ❌ One does not realize how strange it is to be a hairless ape.
- ✅ You don't really notice how weird it is to be a bald ape.

---

**Nhắc:** giữ đúng SỐ DÒNG (1 câu/dòng) và sự thật/số liệu. Nếu bản sau lỡ dài hơn
bản trước nhiều → cắt thêm chữ thừa, đừng để tràn timing.
