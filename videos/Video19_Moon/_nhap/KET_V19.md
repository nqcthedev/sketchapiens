So did they know which nights were safe.
Nobody can prove it.
The one line of evidence anyone ever put forward for ancient people counting nights has not survived being checked.
What survives is narrower, and harder to argue with.
The pattern was real.
It repeated every month.
And it was the largest, slowest, most obvious object in the sky above them.
A group that read it went down to the water on the right nights.
A group that did not paid for that, over and over, for a very long time.
Go back to the overhang one last time.
The same people, the same red smear of a fire, the same flat ground that told them nothing.
Everything that place had to say about the next few hours was overhead.
And it had been saying it on the same schedule long before there was anyone standing there to hear it.
That schedule did not stop.
The moon goes dark for ten nights at a stretch, then comes up late, exactly as it did then.
The only thing that has changed is that nobody stands outside a shelter now, looking up, working out whether tonight is a night for going down to the water.
