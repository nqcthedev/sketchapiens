## KHỐI LỆNH — copy từ đây

You are about to read the narration script of a YouTube documentary video. You have not seen
any notes about it, and that is deliberate.

Read it once, straight through, the way a viewer would hear it. Then answer these questions
in order. Answer from the script alone. If something is not in the script, say it is not in
the script rather than filling the gap with what you already know about the subject.

1. In one sentence, what does this video claim? Not what it is about. What does it argue.

2. What is the evidence for that claim, in the order the script gives it? List it.

3. At what point, if any, did you stop believing the script, or feel it was overselling?
   Quote the line.

4. Where did your attention drop? Quote the first line of the stretch that lost you, and say
   how long the dull stretch runs.

5. Is there anything the script states as fact that it has not supported? Quote it.

6. The script has a section near the end where it lists its own limitations. Does that section
   make you trust the video more, or does it make you feel the video wasted your time? Be blunt.

7. If you had to cut two minutes, which part goes, and why that part.

8. What is the last line doing? Does it land, or does it fade out.

9. Would you have watched to the end. Yes or no, then why.

Do not be encouraging. Do not soften. If it is boring, say which part and why.
