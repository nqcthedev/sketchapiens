# V19 — ĐỢT 1: HOOK *(bản tách, 06/08)*

```
It is the middle of the night, fifty thousand years ago, and you need to pee.
There is no bathroom and no door. Just a ring of orange light, twenty steps across.
Past its edge you see nothing. Everything out there sees you.
So the sensible thing is to wait.
You hold it until sunrise and walk out in daylight.
That is what your own body arranges for you every night of your life.
You sleep eight hours and it does not wake you once.
So why couldn't they?
Not because they were soft, or slow, or hairless, or close to blind after sunset.
All of that is true. None of it is the answer.
The answer is that the system has a failure mode.
It fails hardest on the coldest nights.
And it fails worst in the people least able to survive what was waiting outside.
```
