# V19 — ĐỢT 2: CHƯƠNG 1 — CA ĐÊM

```
Start with the system that works, because you are running it right now.
After sunset two things climb together in your blood.
One is melatonin, which everybody has heard of.
The other is vasopressin, which almost nobody has.
Vasopressin is an instruction to your kidneys, and the instruction is to make less urine.
Your bladder is working the same shift.
It clocks on at sunset and has never once called in sick.
Overnight it turns down the signal it sends your brain, so it can hold more before it says anything.
Your phone does something similar at eleven at night.
Do Not Disturb does not stop the messages arriving. It stops the phone telling you about them.
That is roughly your bladder after dark. Filling, and saying nothing.
Now, here is the part most people get backwards.
The reason this system exists is not predators.
It exists because you cannot drink while you are asleep, so the body stops spending water it has no way to replace.
Nocturnal animals run the same rhythm with the hours reversed.
Water was the reason it evolved. Staying inside all night was a side effect.
But the side effect is the one that mattered.
So what was it worth?
The cleanest way to see that is in the people who do not have it.
When the vasopressin rhythm goes flat, urine production does not drop after dark.
Those people are up three and four times a night, every night.
The condition has a name. Nocturnal polyuria.
So sleeping through the night is not something you do.
It is something that gets done for you.
And fifty thousand years ago it was doing something more than letting you rest.
It was keeping a soft, slow, hairless animal with no night vision on the safe side of the firelight.
Eight hours at a stretch, without that animal having to decide anything.
```
