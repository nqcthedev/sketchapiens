## BẢN DÙNG

```
So here is the answer to the question this started with.
The most dangerous thing ancient humans did was not the hunt.
It was not the mammoth. It was not the winter.
It was twenty steps, in the dark, with your back to the fire, because the body needed something and would not wait.
Every night. For two hundred thousand years.
Long enough for a body to learn it.
You will never take that walk. There is nothing outside your bedroom.
But there is a short walk you have done in your own house with the lights off.
Bathroom and back. Thirty seconds.
And on the way back, you walked faster.
You did not decide to. You just did.
Nothing in your house has ever chased you.
That little burst of speed is a receipt.
Somebody paid for it, and it was not you.
So tonight, coming back through the dark, watch what your legs do near the end.
They hurry.
On a floor you have crossed ten thousand times, in a house where nothing has ever happened.
That is not fear. Fear would be useful.
That is change left over from a fire somebody else sat up all night to keep alight.
And you are spending it on a cold floor, on the way back to bed.
```

## Đối chiếu PHẦN G — công thức kết bài triết học

| Nhịp | Yêu cầu | Dòng |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hành động **vật lý** người xem sắp làm, không phải ý niệm | *"tonight, coming back through the dark, watch what your legs do"* |
| 2 | Nối hành động đó vào chuỗi tổ tiên | *"change left over from a fire somebody else sat up all night to keep alight"* |
| 3 | Đóng bằng nốt **mất mát**, câu cuối chứa **vật thể cụ thể** | *"a cold floor, on the way back to bed"* — sàn lạnh + giường |

Theo mẫu mạnh nhất của PHẦN G *(bài Nấu ăn)*: **chi tiết cuối càng tầm thường càng phiền toái thì cú hạ cánh càng mạnh.** Ở đây là bàn chân trần trên sàn lạnh lúc nửa đêm.

**Bookend:** *"twenty steps"* + *"back to the fire"* gọi lại nguyên xi hook.
**Chùm câu cực ngắn** trước cú đóng: *"They hurry."* (2) → *"That is not fear."* (4) → *"Fear would be useful."* (4).
