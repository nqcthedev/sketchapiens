## PHẦN DÁN VÀO Ô MÔ TẢ

```
How Did Ancient Humans Survive Months of Being Wet?

Nine weeks of rain. No boots, no roof, no dry clothes. This is what actually
happens to a human body that never gets dry, and the four things people worked
out to stay alive through it.

HOW THIS WAS MADE

I picked this topic, read the papers, and wrote the script myself. Where the
evidence only goes so far, I say so in the video instead of rounding it up into
a better story. Every source is listed below so you can check any of it.

If I got something wrong, tell me in the comments and I will pin the correction.


📚 RESEARCH & SOURCES

▸ TRENCH FOOT / IMMERSION FOOT
  Cold immersion foot can damage a foot within 12-14 hours; the warm-water
  form takes roughly 48-72 hours. Mechanism: hyperhydration of the stratum
  corneum leads to maceration, blistering, and skin loss.
  Standard wilderness and military medicine references.

▸ WET HEAT LOSS
  A wet body loses heat up to 25x faster than a dry one — water conducts heat
  far better than air. Standard wilderness medicine reference.

▸ SIBUDU CAVE, SOUTH AFRICA — 77,000-YEAR-OLD BEDDING
  Fifteen separate layers of compacted sedge and rush bedding, dated 77,000 to
  38,000 years ago. The uppermost layers are topped with a tissue-thin layer of
  Cryptocarya woodii (Cape laurel) leaves, which contain insecticidal and
  larvicidal compounds.
  Wadley, Sievers, Bamford, Goldberg, Berna & Miller, "Middle Stone Age Bedding
  Construction and Settlement Patterns at Sibudu, South Africa", Science, 334,
  9 December 2011. DOI: 10.1126/science.1213317

▸ WEST AFRICAN RAINFOREST OCCUPATION — 150,000 YEARS AGO
  Evidence that humans lived in West African rainforest (Côte d'Ivoire), rather
  than passing through it. Published 2026.

▸ MBUTI FORAGING ECOLOGY, EASTERN CONGO
  For roughly five months of the year, essentially none of the calorically
  important forest fruits and seeds are available. Honey is scarce in the same
  window. (Modern ethnographic data, not an archaeological claim.)

▸ FALLBACK FOODS
  Foragers across tropical Africa, Asia and South America rely on underground
  storage organs — roots, tubers, rhizomes — when above-ground foods fail.

▸ RAINFALL AND INSECT-BORNE DISEASE
  Unusually heavy rainfall is followed by sharp increases in biting-insect
  populations and in conditions favourable to vector-borne disease.

▸ TROPICAL SOIL AND ARCHAEOLOGICAL PRESERVATION
  Acidic, biologically active rainforest soils destroy most organic remains.
  Ash and charcoal can be blown or washed away. Dry caves preserve; wet
  seasons do not.

▸ OLDEST FIRE SITES — KENYA AND SOUTH AFRICA
  Approximately 1.6-1.4 million years ago. Open-air site in Kenya, cave site
  in South Africa. Evidence indicates fire was collected from lightning
  strikes and wildfires, not made.

▸ FIRE KEEPING AS A FULL-TIME ROLE
  Maintaining a fire required dedicated attention from at least one group
  member, who would not be available for hunting or gathering.

▸ BARNHAM, SUFFOLK — ~400,000-YEAR-OLD HEARTH
  A patch of heated clay, flint hand axes, and two small pieces of iron pyrite.
  Struck against flint, pyrite throws sparks. The excavators read this as
  deliberate fire-MAKING rather than fire collection, pushing the earliest firm
  evidence back by roughly 350,000 years.
  Published in Nature, December 2025. Excavation reported by the British Museum,
  the Natural History Museum and UCL.

▸ ÖTZI THE ICEMAN — ~5,300 YEARS OLD
  Carried a fire kit: flint, iron pyrite, and dried tinder fungus
  (Fomes fomentarius), which catches a spark and holds an ember for hours.


⚠️ WHAT THIS VIDEO DOES NOT CLAIM

The Sibudu leaves are evidence that people knew certain plants repel insects
and used that knowledge deliberately, fifteen times over. Nobody can prove
those leaves were specifically a wet-season measure. The video says so.

The Mbuti figures are modern ethnographic data from one forest people. They
are not a measurement of the Palaeolithic.

The scene-setting ("it's fifty thousand years ago, and it has been raining for
nine weeks") is a framing device, not an archaeological claim.

```

## KHỐI "HOW THIS WAS MADE" — VÌ SAO CÓ

Chính sách hỏi *"có người thật với góc nhìn thật đứng sau kênh không"*. Đây là câu trả lời **bằng chữ**, không cần nói một tiếng nào:

- *"I picked this topic, read the papers, and wrote the script myself"* — nói rõ tác giả là ai
- *"Where the evidence only goes so far, I say so instead of rounding it up into a better story"* — đây là **thái độ**, thứ không copy được
- *"If I got something wrong, tell me and I will pin the correction"* — mời đối chất, kênh farm không bao giờ dám

Ba câu này + khối "WHAT THIS VIDEO DOES NOT CLAIM" bên dưới là toàn bộ phần "giọng người thật" của kênh. **Không cần thu âm.**
