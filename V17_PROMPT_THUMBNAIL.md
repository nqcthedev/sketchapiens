# V17 — PROMPT THUMBNAIL (dán thẳng)
*Bản v2. Viết lại sau khi soi 15 thumbnail của Mack.*

**Chữ trên ảnh:** `ROTTING?`
**Tỉ lệ:** chọn **16:9** trong giao diện
**Ảnh tham chiếu:** đính kèm 1 ảnh người-que bất kỳ trong `Video16_Winter/`
**Gen 4 bản một lượt.**

---

```
FLAT 2D CARTOON — hand-drawn explainer doodle. Absolutely flat colour fills, clean
even black outlines. NO texture. NO gradients on rock or ground. NO ambient shadow.
NO painterly rendering. NO 3D. NO photorealism. 16:9.

ONE CHARACTER ONLY, sitting on a low rock slightly LEFT of centre, in the open, in
daylight rain. A stick figure with a LARGE round WHITE head — the head takes about
30% of the frame height. Messy wet brown hair plastered flat. A small ragged brown
hide around the hips, soaked. Thin black stick limbs.

THE FACE IS THE WHOLE POINT: heavy half-lidded eyes, flat straight mouth, eyebrows
slightly raised in the middle. Bored. Resigned. Utterly done with this. NOT shocked,
NOT frightened, NOT in pain.

WHAT HE IS DOING: he has one bare foot pulled up onto his knee and is calmly
peeling a small pale curl of skin off the sole with two fingers, the way you'd peel
a sticker. The pale sole is turned toward the viewer, wrinkled and puckered. He is
not looking at it with alarm — he is barely looking at all.

ONE PROP, on the RIGHT, well separated from him: a small pile of soaked animal hides
slumped in a puddle, water running off them.

BACKGROUND — keep it nearly empty: a bright pale grey-white rainy sky filling the
upper half, a flat brown muddy ground, thin vertical rain lines across the whole
frame, one shallow puddle. Nothing else. Lots of empty space.

CONTRAST RULE: the white head and the pale foot must be the brightest things in the
picture and must stand out sharply from the mud and the grey sky.

TEXT: bold bright-YELLOW all-caps rounded marker text reading "ROTTING?" with a
thick black outline, across the TOP of the frame, about 17% of the frame height and
about 55% of the frame width, over the pale sky.

DO NOT INCLUDE: blood, wounds, gore, red colour anywhere, a second character, a
cave, a fire, a mushroom, extra props, a second text block, an exclamation mark, any
character looking off-frame, texture or shading on surfaces, a watermark or logo.
```

# SOI 5 MỤC SAU KHI GEN

| # | Kiểm | Hỏng thì |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mặt có **nửa mí chán đời** không | ra mặt hoảng → sai tông cả lane, làm lại |
| 2 | Đầu có **to ~30% chiều cao khung** không | nhỏ hơn → chết ở cỡ feed |
| 3 | Có bị **vân đá / bóng đổ** không | có → lệch style kênh, không khớp 185 ảnh trong video |
| 4 | Đếm đồ vật | quá **1 người + 1 đống da** → quá tải |
| 5 | **Thu về 168×94** | không thấy đầu trắng + bàn chân nhợt → làm lại concept |

Gen xong gửi ảnh vào chat, tôi thu nhỏ chấm giúp.
