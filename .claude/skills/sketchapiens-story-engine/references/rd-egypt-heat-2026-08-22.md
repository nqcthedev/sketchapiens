# 2. MACRO STORY MAP — BẢN ĐỒ CÂU CHUYỆN

The script does not function primarily as a horizontal list of survival tricks.
It establishes one compact explanatory model, then moves through multiple implementation domains.

```text
HOOK / INVISIBLE ACHIEVEMENT REFRAME
Ancient Egypt is usually remembered for pyramids / mummies / gold.
The hidden engineering achievement is keeping a large population functioning in extreme heat.

↓

MASTER PHYSIOLOGICAL MODEL
reduce heat load
+ replace water
+ replace salt

↓

IMPLEMENTATIONS ACROSS DOMAINS
architecture / thermal mass
→ passive ventilation
→ linen / body covering
→ beer / hydration claim
→ salt / diet claim
→ work timing / labor organization
→ medicine

↓

LATE REFRAME / SCALE-OUT
The desert is not only the threat.
It also becomes border / preservation environment / ingredient in mummification logic.

↓

RETURN TO MODERN HUMAN
same species / same heat problem / different built environment
```

The important structural observation is that many chapters are **parallel implementations of one model**, not a strict chain where each solution causes the next problem.

# 3. OBSERVATION O-EGYPT-01 — UNIFYING EQUATION

**Status:** `observation` — chưa tạo candidate riêng.

## Surface pattern

The script explicitly compresses the survival problem into three operations:

```text
reduce heat load
replace water
replace salt
```

The later chapters are then interpreted as different implementations of that equation.

## Why it may matter

A long explainer can remain coherent across large Domain Shifts when the viewer has a compact model that answers:

> "Why does this next domain still belong to the same question?"

This may allow:

```text
physiology
→ architecture
→ airflow physics
→ textiles
→ food / drink
→ chemistry
→ labor organization
→ medicine
→ geopolitics
→ preservation / archaeology
```

without requiring a fabricated causal handoff at every boundary.

## What this observation does NOT prove

- every long explainer needs an explicit equation;
- three variables are optimal;
- named formulas improve retention;
- this mechanism caused the video's performance;
- every chapter must map one-to-one to one variable.

## Candidate decision

**Do not create a new mechanism yet.**
Cross-check first against other strong explainers and Sketchapiens scripts.
If this is only a special case of a strong `explanatory model`, a new mechanism name adds no diagnostic power.

# 6. M-002 CONSTRAINT MIGRATION — IMPORTANT NEGATIVE / DISTINCTION CASE

Egypt Heat should NOT be counted lazily as a clean M-002 positive.

Many transitions are approximately:

```text
architecture helps heat
then clothing also helps heat
then hydration also helps heat
then work timing also helps heat
```

This is not the same as:

```text
solution A works
→ because it works, bottleneck B becomes the new limiting constraint
→ solution B becomes necessary
```

Therefore Egypt Heat is useful as a **distinction test**:

> Coherence can come from a shared explanatory model even when Constraint Migration is weak or absent.

This supports the Story Engine guardrail:

> Do not invent a flaw in one solution merely to force Causal Debt / Constraint Migration into the next chapter.

**M-002 status remains:** `candidate`.

# 11. E-CASE-01 — TRUE FACT, WRONG CAUSAL ROLE

## Script move

The script discusses Nile-water danger and invokes schistosomiasis near the argument that drinking beer was safer than drinking river water.

## External verification

CDC material describes human schistosomiasis infection as acquired through **skin contact with contaminated freshwater**, including wading, swimming, bathing, or washing.

Sources:

- CDC Emerging Infectious Diseases, schistosomiasis etymologia: https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/13/10/e1-1310_article
- CDC traveler health exposure guidance: https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/vietnam

## Evidence lesson

A fact can be correct:

```text
schistosomiasis existed / freshwater exposure mattered
```

while being placed in a narrative slot that suggests a different causal route:

```text
drink river water
→ schistosomiasis risk
→ beer solves that drinking risk
```

This is a strong candidate fixture type for Phase 4:

> **Correct fact, wrong causal role.**

# 12. E-CASE-02 — INTERPRETATION STATED AS CERTAINTY

## Script move

A roof feature in a Nebamun/Theban representation is described as essentially unmistakable evidence of a wind scoop / malkaf.

## External verification

Oxford research discusses representations interpreted as wind-catchers and explicitly considers multiple representational / functional possibilities, including artistic symmetry and different airflow arrangements.

Source:

- Oxford University Research Archive, *Perceptions of meteorological phenomena in ancient Egypt*: https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3Ad0e387f0-084f-4b0e-b539-4ecef7fdac87

## Evidence lesson

Potential inflation path:

```text
scholarly interpretation
→ "probably / may represent"
→ script certainty
→ "unmistakably"
```

Phase-4 fixture type:

> **Interpretation → certainty inflation.**

# 13. E-CASE-03 — EVENT IS REAL, STORY USE IS STRONGER THAN EVENT RECORD

## Script move

The Deir el-Medina strike is used to support a larger causal synthesis in which beer/rations function as the workforce's heat-management / hydration system.

## External verification

Museo Egizio's Strike Papyrus material states that workers protested after suspended/delayed rations and specifically records requests for **grain rations / provisions**, along with hunger and thirst.

Sources:

- Museo Egizio, Strike Papyrus highlight: https://collezionepapiri.museoegizio.it/en-GB/section/Papyrus-Collection/History-and-content/Highlights-Turin-Strike-Papyrus/
- Museo Egizio, Cat. 1880: https://collezioni.museoegizio.it/en-GB/material/Cat_1880/

The record establishes a real strike and ration crisis.
It does not by itself establish the stronger synthesis:

```text
beer ration = cooling system
ration delay = switching off the water supply
```

## Evidence lesson

Phase-4 fixture type:

> **Real event, unsupported causal upgrade.**

The bridge may still be arguable if independent evidence supports it, but the event record alone cannot carry the full story claim.

# 14. E-CASE-04 — PLAUSIBLE SYNTHESIS PRESENTED AS OPTIMIZATION

## Script move

The script combines:

```text
sweat loses sodium
+ salted / preserved foods
+ fruit / beer
```

into a claim that the diet produced approximately the fluid/mineral balance required for heavy labor in extreme heat.

## Evidence classification

Even if each ingredient-level fact is true, the combined conclusion is a **synthesis** unless direct evidence demonstrates the physiological adequacy of that diet for the stated labor conditions.

Phase-4 fixture type:

> **A + B + C are individually supported; A+B+C → optimization is not automatically supported.**

This is a key M-004 target because ordinary fact-checking can mark every component TRUE while still missing the unsupported bridge.

# 15. E-CASE-05 — ABSOLUTE LANGUAGE OVERSHOOTS A REAL PATTERN

## Script move

The desert is described as a natural defensive barrier, then escalated toward absolute claims that armies / supply lines cannot cross it.

## External verification

A scholarly overview supports the broad point that Egypt's deserts and geography formed a **natural barrier from foreign invasions**.

Source:

- Koyano, *The Concept of Borders or Frontiers, and Their Geographical Locations in Ancient Egypt*: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jorient1962/44/1/44_1_1/_article

The broad barrier claim does not justify a universal impossibility claim.

## Evidence lesson

Phase-4 fixture type:

> **Strong tendency / barrier → absolute impossibility.**

---

# 16. E-CASE-06 — DIRECT FACTUAL OVERCLAIM IN A STRONG HOOK

## Script move

The hook states that living to around 60 was effectively the oldest anyone lived in the ancient world.

## External verification

Bioarchaeological work documents ancient Egyptian/Nubian individuals estimated into their late 70s and cites evidence of people living into their 80s; Ramesses II is commonly estimated above 90.

Source:

- Buzon, *Growing Old at Tombos: A View of Older Adults in an Ancient Egyptian-Nubian Community*: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ajpa.70099

## Evidence lesson

This is not a subtle synthesis problem.
It is a reminder that a highly polished opening can contain a simple factual overclaim.

Phase-4 fixture type:

> **Hook compression → false maximum / universal statement.**

# 17. M-004 EVIDENCE FIT — WHAT THIS CASE ADDS

Egypt Heat provides multiple distinct failure shapes for M-004:

```text
1. correct fact, wrong causal role
2. interpretation stated as certainty
3. real event used to prove a stronger causal story
4. individually supported facts combined into unsupported optimization
5. real tendency escalated into absolute impossibility
6. direct factual overclaim in hook compression
```

This strengthens the case that Evidence verification needs to ask more than:

> "Is each sentence individually true?"

It may need to ask:

> "Does this evidence prove the exact causal / functional / universal role the narrative assigns to it?"

**M-004 status remains `candidate`.**
Do not promote before checking whether the existing Evidence Prosecutor already catches these patterns reliably.
